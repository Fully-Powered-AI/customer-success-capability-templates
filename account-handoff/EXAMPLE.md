# Account handoff: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* An invented 80-person managed IT services company has just signed Buyer A, an invented 300-person professional-services firm.

**Account handoff: Buyer A**

- **Sold:** Business-day help desk 7:00 to 19:00 Pacific, laptop management for 300 devices, starter and leaver setup within one business day, monthly report, on-site support within four hours at two offices. 24-month term. Excluded: phone system, cabling. (Agreement and order form, 13 October 2026)
- **Promised:** Independent security attestation report by 31 March 2027 (agreement, clause 9.4). Next-business-day on-site support at the other two offices (proposal section 4.3; outside the agreement, sales lead to confirm).
- **Buyer A people:** Operations director: signer and sponsor. IT director: day-to-day contact. Procurement lead: contract questions. Office managers: not yet known.
- **Our people:** Client success manager, account executive, solutions lead, finance reviewer.
- **Dates:** Service starts 2 November 2026 (agreement). Kickoff: requested by Buyer A for the week of 26 October, not confirmed.
- **Billing as agreed:** Monthly in advance, per-device fees per order form schedule A, net 30. First invoice 2 November.
- **Risks the seller heard:** New starters waited up to a week under the previous provider; the IT director will judge the first month on this (call notes, 16 September). Administrator access must come from the previous provider, whose contract runs to 31 December.
- **Discrepancy:** Up to 320 devices in the proposal, 300 on the order form. The order form governs. The account executive confirms the count with Buyer A.
- **Missing:** Accounts payable contact. Office manager contacts. Administrator access date.
- **Next actions:** 1. Confirm the device count; get the accounts payable contact, office manager contacts and administrator access date: account executive, 16 October. 2. Confirm the on-site commitment at the two outer offices: sales lead, 16 October. 3. Confirm the kickoff date: client success manager, 20 October. 4. Check billing setup against the order form: finance reviewer, 23 October. 5. Add the report date to the account plan: solutions lead, 30 October.

Prepared for the client success manager’s review. Nothing has been sent to Buyer A and no system has been changed.
