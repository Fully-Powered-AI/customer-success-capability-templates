# Account handoff

The day a deal closes, your client success manager gets one record: what was sold and promised, who is involved, the dates and billing terms as agreed, the risks the seller heard and what is still missing. Every next action has an owner.

*Workflow template · Customer success and account management · version 0.1.0 · [Workflows Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/workflow templates/account-handoff/)*

## The outcome

Client success starts every new account knowing what the seller knows. The kickoff opens with the plan, and the client never repeats what they told sales.

A requested date stays a request until your side confirms it. Where the proposal, order and notes disagree, the record says so and names who resolves it.

## Who starts the work, and when

The account executive starts it the day the contract is signed. The client success manager reviews the record, accepts or reassigns each next action and runs the kickoff. The finance or contracts reviewer confirms billing terms. The sales lead answers for anything promised outside the contract. The AI assistant prepares the record. It never contacts the client, changes billing or updates your systems.

## What they supply

- **The signed agreement and order form.** The authority for what was sold, dates and billing.
- **The final proposal and its evidence checklist,** or the approved account brief.
- **Call notes and emails from the sale.** What the client was told and what the seller heard.
- **Known contacts,** with roles.
- **Your onboarding checklist.** The steps you run for this kind of client.

## What comes out

1.  **The handoff record.** Sold, promised, people, dates, billing terms and risks, each with its source.
2.  **Discrepancies and gaps.** Where sources disagree, and what the onboarding checklist needs that nobody supplied.
3.  **Next actions.** One owner and one due date each.

Good looks like this: the client success manager could run the kickoff from the record alone, and every open item has a name beside it.

## What has to change for your company

- **The authority order.** Which document wins when sources disagree.
- **The handoff moment.** The event that starts the work, and how many days the record has.
- **Owners by role.** Who confirms billing, who answers for side promises and who owns the account.
- **Your platform.** It runs in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini. Decide which deal records the assistant may read.

## Start using it

1. Read [EXAMPLE.md](EXAMPLE.md) to see what a good result looks like.
2. Open [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md) and set every item under "What to customize" to your own rules, roles and limits.
3. Give your AI assistant the instruction block and the output format from the template, with your own limits in place of the ones the instructions leave open in square brackets. Paste them in as custom instructions in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini, or copy this whole folder into a product that loads `SKILL.md` packages. This folder stands alone; you do not need the rest of the repository.
4. Run it on past work first, using the reviewer checklist and acceptance tests in the template, before anyone relies on it.
