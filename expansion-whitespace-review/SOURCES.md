# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. The shape of the work draws on two published methods. From anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins (Apache-2.0): reviewing one account in depth or sweeping a whole book; setting what a customer already has against what it could add across products, teams, locations and volume; counting a gap only when something on record backs it; ranking plays on the strength of that evidence, a size drawn from the seller's own usual deal values rather than guessed, and whether anyone already knows the people involved; an approach for each play that builds on a success the customer already has; keeping unsupported ideas apart with the signal that would raise them; each account's best play in a book view; and opportunity records proposed with their evidence and created only when a person accepts them. From LeadMagic/gtm-skills (MIT): the signals that usually point to expansion (usage nearing a plan limit, a request that only a higher tier answers, more people joining the customer's team, a new need raised in a review), the kinds of play (more seats, a higher tier, a further product, wider rollout), not raising expansion before a new customer has had value from onboarding, and retention coming before expansion for a customer at risk. Fully Powered AI added the three evidence levels and the nine-point score, the rule that estimates come from your catalogue and a recorded quantity or not at all, turning those two cautions into holds with a fixed length, an end date and the health verdict that triggers them, confirming the need before anything is proposed on light evidence, the stop on any export column or offer it cannot match, the record gaps, the quoting of instructions found in notes, and the rule that a person decides, approves and creates every record. No third-party text is reused.

- **Fully Powered AI:** Fully Powered AI's own rules for prepared work: the organization's own catalogue and settings as the only authority, evidence graded and quoted with its source, every next step with one owner and one due date, and a person who decides, approves and creates every record.
