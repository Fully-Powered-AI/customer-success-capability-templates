# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words, from its own working guide for handing a working process over to a customer and reading how the first weeks go. The shape of the work also draws on five published packages from four repositories, all under the MIT licence. From coreyhaines31/marketingskills: naming the moment of first value before planning any step, listing every step that leads to it and keeping only what that moment needs, and a short list that opens with a step the customer can finish at once. From CALLE-AI/awesome-phone-call-agents: sorting promises into dated, undated and dependent on the other side, counting only your own side's promises as your commitments, and treating anything the record does not support as unknown rather than true. From deciqAI/knowledge-skills: ending the onboarding on the customer's own success, with something to keep and a date ahead. From swan-gtm/gtm-skills: counting a setup step only when the customer did it deliberately, stopping the plan when the account goes silent, and never forcing a case into the nearest category. Fully Powered AI added the first real use as the goal, milestones a person can be seen doing, the commitments with their owners, the messages held for approval, and the honest fix for each reason a new customer stalls. No third-party text is reused.
