# Fully Powered Capability Templates: Customer Success

Clients who never have to repeat themselves. These capability templates take the handoffs and updates that hold an account together and give an AI assistant a precise way to prepare them, so every client hears from a team that knows the whole story.

This repository holds 10 capability templates for customer success and account management teams. Each one is a single piece of work with its own folder: what it is for and who starts it, the complete template an AI assistant follows, a worked example with invented data, what to change for your company, what has been checked, and its sources.

## The capabilities

- **[Account commitments check](account-commitments-check/)**: Before the next customer conversation, the account manager gets one list of what each side promised in recent meetings and emails, each linked to its source and marked open, done or unclear, with a suggested owner. Nothing goes to the customer.
- **[Account handoff](account-handoff/)**: The day a deal closes, your client success manager gets one record: what was sold and promised, who is involved, the dates and billing terms as agreed, the risks the seller heard and what is still missing. Every next action has an owner.
- **[Churn risk and save plan](churn-risk-and-save-plan/)**: Once a quarter, the owner or account lead sees why customers really left, read from your own cancellation notes and records, how many left for each cause, and one change to try for each, with an owner, a start date and the count that will show whether it helped. A person decides every change.
- **[Client-status update preparation](client-status-update-preparation/)**: Every client gets an accurate status update on schedule, drafted from the notes and the task list, with confirmed dates kept apart from estimates and the client’s open decisions in plain view.
- **[Customer onboarding plan](customer-onboarding-plan/)**: For every new customer, your customer success lead gets one plan for the first weeks: the result the customer must reach, the fewest milestones that get them there with an owner on each side, the promises both sides made, and each message as a draft. Nothing is sent until a person approves it.
- **[Expansion whitespace review](expansion-whitespace-review/)**: For one account or a whole book, the account manager sees what each customer holds against what it could also use, the evidence for every gap, the plays ranked, and one next step and owner for each. A person decides what to propose; nothing goes into the sales system without approval.
- **[Feedback follow-through updates](feedback-follow-through-updates/)**: When a change ships or a decision lands on something customers asked for, your account owner gets the list of who asked, the confirmed status of each request, and an individual draft update for each person in the channel they prefer. No update promises a date or feature the team has not confirmed.
- **[Lapsed-customer win-back](lapsed-customer-win-back/)**: Once a month, the owner or account manager sees which regular customers have gone far longer than usual without ordering, ranked by what each bought in their last active year, with open complaints set aside for a call and a short note drafted for the rest, using only approved offers. A person approves every note.
- **[Quarterly business review preparation](quarterly-business-review-preparation/)**: Before each customer's quarterly review, the account manager gets a pack built on the customer's own goals: the quarter's result for each, labelled by where it came from, what is still open, next quarter's goals for discussion and a timed agenda. A person settles every question and sends it.
- **[Renewal risk review](renewal-risk-review/)**: Once a month, the account manager sees every contract coming up for renewal on one page: the dates that matter, the warning signs your organization watches for, which accounts are at risk, and the next step for each with an owner and a date. A person decides and makes every contact.

## How to use one

Open a folder and start with its README. Every folder stands alone, so copy the one you want and leave the rest. The templates are written for ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot and Gemini: paste the instruction block and the output format in as custom instructions, or load the folder in a product that reads `SKILL.md` packages.

In every template a person starts the work, supplies the inputs and decides. The template prepares and checks. Anything that sends, posts, pays or changes a record waits for a person to approve it.

## What "checked" means here

Each folder says exactly what was checked and when: who wrote it, that its sources and rights were reviewed, the date of its editorial review, and whether it has been tried on its invented example in a named AI product. Nothing is described as tested for a client unless it was. Test a template on your own past work before your team relies on it; each one comes with acceptance tests for that.
