# Quarterly business review preparation

Before each customer's quarterly review, the account manager gets a pack built on the customer's own goals: the quarter's result for each, labelled by where it came from, what is still open, next quarter's goals for discussion and a timed agenda. A person settles every question and sends it.

*Workflow template · Customer success and account management · version 0.1.0 · [Workflows Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/workflow templates/quarterly-business-review-preparation/)*

## The outcome

The review is about what the customer set out to achieve, not about how busy their team has been in the product. Every goal the customer agreed appears in their own words, with its baseline, its target and the quarter's result, and a plain status: met, progressing, not moving, not measured, or figures that disagree. Each result says where it came from: the customer's own report, your own system, or something a person at the customer said. Nothing in the pack is a figure the record does not hold.

How much the customer's team uses the product still has a place, in its own section, kept apart from the results. Last review's promises on both sides are checked, the open issues are listed, and next quarter's goals and commitments arrive as a draft to agree in the room. A timed agenda opens with the customer's priorities and names anyone missing from the invitation, such as the owner of a goal or a senior sponsor who has not taken part for a while.

The account manager walks in with the questions already settled: which of two figures counts, why a goal went unmeasured. The AI assistant prepares the pack; it sends nothing, issues no invitation, changes no record and puts no price in front of the customer.

## Who starts the work, and when

The account manager or customer success manager who owns the customer starts it about two weeks before the quarterly review, once the quarter's figures are in. It runs again when a missing figure arrives or the attendee list changes. The head of customer success, or whoever leads the accounts, reviews the pack before the account manager sends it to the customer.

## What they supply

- **The success plan:** the goals the customer agreed, each with its measure, baseline, target and the person at the customer who owns it.
- **The results log,** as a spreadsheet: one row per figure, with the goal, the value, the date it describes, where it came from and whether it is the customer's figure or yours.
- **The settings:** the quarter, the review date and length, who is invited from each side and the customer's senior sponsor.
- **Where they have them:** what the customer's team is and is not using, notes from the quarter's calls, last review's commitments, open support issues, and the goals or topics the account manager wants to propose.

## What comes out

One pack in a fixed order. At the top, the quarter in brief: how many goals are met, progressing, not moving or unmeasured, and one line for each goal that moved forward. Then every goal with its result, its source label, its status and, where the customer said something about it, their words.

After the results come the customer's use of the product, the open items from last review and from support, changes among the customer's people, next quarter's goals and commitments for discussion, a timed agenda with who is invited and who is not, and the questions to settle before the meeting, each with the person who settles it. Anything in the notes that reads as an instruction, such as a request to add a price, is quoted for the account manager and left undone.

## What has to change for your company

Write each customer's goals down with a baseline, a target and an owner on the customer's side; the pack is only as good as the success plan. Keep a results log, or match your export's column names to the ones the template expects, once. Decide whether your own figures may stand alone or must be confirmed by the customer before the pack goes out. Set the review length, the agenda and how many days before the meeting the pack is sent. Name who reviews the pack and who approves anything commercial. It runs in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini from the files you supply; it needs no connection to your CRM.
