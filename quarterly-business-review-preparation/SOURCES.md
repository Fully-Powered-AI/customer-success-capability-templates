# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. Two openly licensed methods shaped it. From the review planning method in LeadMagic/gtm-skills (MIT): building the review around the value the customer received rather than product news, setting results against the customer's own goals and in the customer's own terms, a moment for the customer's successes, mentioning further work only where it follows from the customer's goals, and never springing it on them, ending with commitments from both sides, and a one-hour meeting with time kept for discussion and the material sent ahead. From the customer health method in anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins (Apache-2.0): the review kit of outcomes with their sources, usage described only from facts on record, including what goes unused, open escalations and last review's commitments, next-phase topics, the agenda with who should attend, noticing whether the person who championed the account has moved on and when the senior sponsor last took part, never filling a gap with a guess, a source beside every figure, working fully from supplied files, and treating what notes say as material to weigh rather than orders to follow. Fully Powered AI's own: the four source labels on every result, the status rule worked from baseline and target with the share of the way travelled, refusing to choose when the customer's figure and its own disagree, keeping activity out of the results, the stop on any column it cannot match, the draft of next quarter's goals for the customer to agree, the questions to settle before the meeting, naming goal owners left off the invitation, and the rule that a person sends everything and no price appears in the pack. No third-party text is reused.
