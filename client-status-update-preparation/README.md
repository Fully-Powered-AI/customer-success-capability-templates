# Client-status update preparation

Every client gets an accurate status update on schedule, drafted from the notes and the task list, with confirmed dates kept apart from estimates and the client’s open decisions in plain view.

*Workflow template · Customer success and account management · version 0.1.0 · [Workflows Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/workflow templates/client-status-update-preparation/)*

## The outcome

A fully powered client team sends every client a clear update on the day it is due, and every line in it is true. The client sees what moved, which dates are committed, which are still estimates and which decisions only the client can make. Each lead can run 10X the client relationships with the same care.

This workflow template drafts that update from the matter or project notes, the task list and the last update sent. The person who owns the client relationship reviews, edits and sends it. The draft never goes out on its own and never turns an estimate into a promise.

## Who starts the work, and when

A project manager, responsible attorney, account director or engagement lead starts it on the agreed update day, and again between updates when a date moves or a new decision lands on the client’s side.

## What they supply

- **The notes since the last update.** Meeting notes, call notes and internal comments.
- **The task list or plan.** Each date with its status: confirmed, estimated or at risk.
- **The last update sent.** What the client was told to expect.
- **The update standard.** Length, greeting, sign-off, section order and what never goes in.

## What comes out

A draft message covering progress, confirmed dates, labelled estimates, client decisions with a needed-by date and the next step, plus a note for the reviewer. The example is an invented 30-person civil engineering firm designing site drainage for a client’s warehouse expansion.

Good means the client knows what to do after one read, and the project manager sees every judgment the draft left for them.

## What has to change for your company

1.  **What counts as confirmed.** For example, only a date the client agreed in writing.
2.  **Cadence and triggers.** The update day for each client, and what calls for one in between.
3.  **What never goes in.** Internal staffing and fee discussions. In a law firm, advice and legal deadlines stay with the responsible attorney.
4.  **Review and sending.** Who reviews each client’s update and who sends it.

We start from what the client should know after reading, map the work from notes to sent message, and land each step on its owner. This workflow template is written for ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot and Gemini, and your firm keeps control of how the work gets done.
