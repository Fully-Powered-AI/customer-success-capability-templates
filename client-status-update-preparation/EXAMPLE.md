# Client-status update preparation: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* The sources on update day.

| Source | What it says |
|----|----|
| Last update, 2 November | Drainage layout to Client C on 6 November. Planning submission targeted for 20 November. |
| Task list, 9 November | Layout sent 6 November. Planning submission 20 November: confirmed with Client C’s planning consultant. Final drawings 11 December: estimate. |
| Project notes, 9 November | Both detention-tank locations work. Client C must choose by 13 November to hold the 11 December estimate. Internal: drafting team shared with another project in December. |

*Illustrative example with invented data.* The draft update.

```
Subject: Project 7731 status, week of 9 November

Since our update on 2 November, we sent the drainage
layout on 6 November, as planned.

Confirmed: the planning submission goes in on
20 November. Your planning consultant has agreed it.

Estimate: final construction drawings by 11 December.
This date depends on the decision below.

Your decision, needed by 13 November: which of the two
detention-tank locations to use. Both work. A choice by
13 November keeps the 11 December estimate.

Next step: we prepare the planning submission and
confirm the drawings date once you choose.

[Project manager]
```

*Illustrative example with invented data.* The note for the reviewer.

**Reviewer note: Project 7731 update**

- **Changed:** Layout delivered on the promised date. Planning submission moved from target to confirmed.
- **Dates:** 20 November: confirmed. 11 December: estimate. 13 November: from project notes; confirm before sending.
- **Left out on purpose:** The shared drafting team, an internal note. It bears on the 11 December estimate; decide whether the client should hear it.

Nothing has been sent. The project manager reviews, edits and sends.
