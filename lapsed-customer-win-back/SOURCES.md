Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. From the win-back method in anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins (Apache-2.0) it takes these ideas: judge each customer by their own habit instead of an average, order the list by past spending instead of by how long each customer has been away, check that the order dates are real before working out any gap, send a customer with a complaint to a phone call instead of a note, have a person confirm the list and the offer before anything is written, use only offers the owner has agreed, open with an honest question about the pause rather than a sales pitch, and build each note from facts in the customer's own orders. From the win-back guide in finsilabs/awesome-ecommerce-skills (MIT) it takes counting a customer as lapsed after twice their usual gap between orders, matching the offer to how much the customer has spent, keeping any discount out of the opening, waiting a set period before writing to the same customer again, and checking consent and recent orders before anything is sent. Fully Powered AI added the middle-of-recent-gaps rule for the usual gap, the 30-day floor, the slipping and long-gone groups, the stop on any export column it cannot match, the approved-offer list with value bands, the reasons every left-out customer is listed, the record gaps, and the rule that a person approves and sends every note. No third-party text is reused.

- **Fully Powered AI:** Fully Powered AI's own rules for prepared work: each customer's usual gap as the middle of their recent gaps, a floor below which nobody counts as lapsed, the slipping and long-gone groups, an approved-offer list with value bands as the only source of offers, every left-out customer listed with its reason, and a person who approves and sends every note.
