# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words, from its own working guide for reading why clients drift away. Published methods shaped parts of that guide and this template, and are credited here for their ideas. coreyhaines31/marketingskills (MIT), its churn-prevention method and its notes on cancellation patterns: keeping customers who chose to leave apart from those lost to a failed payment, a short list of leaving reasons with a catch-all as the starting groups, giving each reason its own response, looking at leavers by plan and by how long they had been customers, making no offer at all to a business that has closed, asking one question with a short list of reasons when cancellations are not collecting them, reminding customers before a card expires, looking for the signs that tend to show up some time before a customer cancels, and reading a customer who leaves in the first weeks as one who never got going, whose remedy is setup help. monte-carlo-data/mc-agent-toolkit (Apache-2.0), its root-cause investigation method and its catalogue of common causes: reading events in date order so that a change that came first is treated as the cause of what followed and fixed first, resting every cause on a record that shows it, showing both explanations when two fit instead of choosing, and saying so when no known pattern fits. CALLE-AI/awesome-phone-call-agents (MIT), its cancellation-respect auditor: the test applied to any change at the moment of cancelling, that the request is carried out where it was made, without obstacles, redirection, fees or guilt, with at most one brief offer, nothing offered again after a refusal, and a plain confirmation. Fully Powered AI's own: the plan as the pattern across leavers rather than a verdict on any current account, the coverage line before any finding, counts with the customers behind them and shares only above a set number of leavers, fair endings recorded as outcomes, one change per cause with one owner, a start-by date, an approver and the count to read next time, early signs read from the leavers' own records, with no claim that they predict leaving, the stop on export columns it cannot match, notes treated as evidence and never as orders, and the rule that a person decides, prices and makes every contact. No third-party text is reused.
