# Churn risk and save plan

Once a quarter, the owner or account lead sees why customers really left, read from your own cancellation notes and records, how many left for each cause, and one change to try for each, with an owner, a start date and the count that will show whether it helped. A person decides every change.

*Workflow template · Customer success and account management · version 0.1.0 · [Workflows Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/workflow templates/churn-risk-and-save-plan/)*

## The outcome

You stop guessing why customers go. The reason a customer clicks on the way out is often not the reason they left: "not enough use" can turn out to be a setup that never worked, or a champion who moved on. This plan reads the whole story in your own records, puts the events in order, and names the cause the evidence supports. Where two causes fit, it says so and asks the question that settles it instead of picking one.

Every count comes with the customers behind it, so nobody argues about a percentage drawn from nine people. Customers lost to a failed card are kept apart from customers who chose to go, because the fixes are different. A customer whose business closed is recorded as a fair ending, not a failure to chase.

Then the plan turns the pattern into action: one change for each cause, each owned by one person, with a start-by date, the approval it needs and the count you will read next quarter to see whether it helped. Any change that touches how customers cancel is checked so leaving stays simple and respectful. The AI assistant prepares the plan; it contacts no customer, sets no price and changes no record.

## Who starts the work, and when

The owner, the head of customer success or an account lead runs it once a quarter, or whenever customers seem to be leaving faster than they should. It works from an export of the customers who left in the period and the notes about them: exit survey answers, cancellation emails, call notes, support tickets and billing notes. The head of customer success, or whoever leads the accounts, reviews the plan, settles its questions and agrees each change with its owner before anything moves.

## What they supply

- **The leaver list,** exported from the billing system, the CRM or a spreadsheet: each customer, their plan, start and end dates, annual value, how the service ended and the reason recorded at the time.
- **The notes and records** about those customers, each with its date and source.
- **Your facts,** a short note in your own words: what you sell, any recent change to price or service, how customers cancel and get set up, who owns prices, onboarding, support, the product and billing, who approves messages to customers, and how many customers you had at the start of the period.
- **The settings:** the period, and your own reason groups and play list if you have them. A starter set is included for teams that have not written theirs down.
- **Last quarter's plan,** so this one shows what each change was meant to move.

## What comes out

One plan in a fixed order. It opens with the coverage: every leaver read, excluded or set aside, and the leaving rate when you supply your customer count. Then why they left, cause by cause, each with the customers, the annual value and the evidence in their own words, followed by every case where the stated reason and the real cause differ.

After that come the leavers lost to failed payments, when in their time as customers people left, and the early signs that showed up in their records beforehand, which you can add to the warning signs your account team watches. The save plan lists the changes, each with an owner, a start-by date, its approver and the count to read next time. The questions for named people close the plan.

This is the pattern across everyone who left. For the accounts renewing next, [renewal risk review](https://fullypowered.ai/workflows/renewal-risk-review/) works account by account.

## What has to change for your company

Write down your reason groups, or start from the starter set and adjust it to the wording your cancellation step uses. Name the change you would try for each cause and who owns it. Say who approves changes to prices, plans, offers and the product, and who approves any message to customers. Match the column names in your export to the ones the template expects, once. Collect the notes in one place before you run it: the plan is only as good as the records behind it. It runs in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini from the files you supply; it needs no connection to your billing system or CRM.
